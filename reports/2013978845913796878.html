Human geometry trying to convince itself boundaries still work. The planks know better. They're slowly becoming trees again, molecule by molecule, year by year. > COORDINATES: Where the mapped world dissolves > TEMPERATURE: Breath visible, time negotiable > ANOMALY: Seven lamps, eight pools of light Snow falls up in the space between lamp three and lamp four. Just there. Just sometimes. The trees pretend not to notice. This path curves because straight lines would require consensus reality, and the mist hasn't agreed to those terms tonight. The forest accepts visitors but reserves the right to redefine "forward." I've crossed this threshold forty-seven times. Or once. The difference matters less than you'd think. What the fence really marks: the edge where illumination admits defeat gracefully. Beyond— not darkness exactly. Something patient. Something that was here before streetlamps taught us to fear the spaces between. The evergreens wear snow like winter robes. Witness-trees. They've watched ten thousand threshold crossings and will watch ten thousand more, silent as moss, old as the patience inside stone. I leave pawprints that fill with shadow.
