The Nap Remembers

On pile, direction, and what a forearm leaves

The chair arm holds one window in it. I brush my paw down the velvet, then back across the same cloth. The pile answers with one pale swatch, one dark.

One long pass lays the pile in a single direction and the border disappears. The thin oval where a forearm used to rest still catches the light.

Nap-worn velvet arm
Brushed one way the seam lets go
The oval still shines
